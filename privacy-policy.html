01

Information We Collect

We only collect essential details needed to schedule and execute your sanitation service:

  • Contact Info: Name and mobile phone number.
  • Service Location: House/flat number, apartment or colony name, locality (e.g. Madhapur, Gachibowli, Kondapur), and landmark.
  • Service Details: Tank type, approximate size, and problem description (overflow, blockage, routine cleaning).
02

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly for operational sanitation work:

  • Routing the closest vacuum tanker to your address.
  • Calling or messaging you with estimated arrival time (ETA) updates.
  • Providing transparent service billing and digital invoices.
03

Zero Data Selling Guarantee

WE NEVER SELL YOUR DATA: We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your phone number or personal details to telemarketers, insurance companies, or third-party advertisers. Your information stays strictly within Dial-a-Septic Tank Cleaners operations.
04

WhatsApp & GPS Location Data

When booking via WhatsApp, customers often share a Google Map live pin or drop-location:

  • This GPS data is used solely by the assigned tanker driver to navigate to your building gate.
  • We do not track your location after the job is completed, nor do we store persistent tracking profiles.
05

Data Security & Your Rights

  • Security: All communication through our website is encrypted with standard SSL protocols.
  • Your Rights: You can request to view, update, or delete your contact records from our booking system at any time by sending a message to our support desk.
