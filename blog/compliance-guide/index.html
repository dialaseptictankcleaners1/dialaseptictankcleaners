1. The Rules Behind Septic Tank Cleaning in Hyderabad

Rule What it means
PEMSR Act, 2013 Hazardous manual cleaning of sewers and septic tanks is prohibited; violations can be prosecuted.
Supreme Court directions, 2023 Outsourced sewer cleaning must not require people to enter sewers; contractors face accountability.
MoHUA Model Contract, 2025 Private operators need trained adult workers, PPE, insurance, minimum wages, logbooks and designated disposal points.
HMWSSB norms Septic vehicles are empanelled, GPS-tracked, and operators trained and supplied with PPE, as reported to the NGT.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is septic tank cleaning regulated in Hyderabad? ▾
Yes. Septic tank and sewer cleaning in Hyderabad is governed by the PEMSR Act 2013, Supreme Court directions (2023), MoHUA Model Contract guidelines (2025), and HMWSSB norms. Hazardous manual cleaning is prohibited by law; private operators must employ trained adult workers, supply full PPE, maintain roadworthy insured vehicles, and dispose of sewage only at designated government treatment plants.
Do you use machines to clean septic tanks? ▾
Yes, 100% mechanised. Dial-a-Septic operates high-vacuum suction tankers and 250-bar hydro-jetting machines. Our crews are strictly never sent into septic tanks or sewer lines, ensuring full compliance with zero manual entry laws.
How often should a septic tank be cleaned? ▾
For individual houses and villas, septic tanks typically require cleaning every 2 to 3 years. For apartment societies, gated communities, and commercial businesses with higher daily usage, tanks should be inspected and cleared every 6 to 12 months to prevent heavy sludge hardening, backflows, and drain chokes.
Is Dial-a-Septic part of HMWSSB? ▾
No. Dial-a-Septic Tank Cleaners is an independent private operator, not part of or endorsed by HMWSSB or any government body. We operate our own fleet of roadworthy tankers and strictly follow all municipal safety guidelines, legal norms, and authorised decanting points.
