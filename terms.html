01

Service Scope

By booking services with Dial-a-Septic Tank Cleaners via phone, WhatsApp, or website, you agree to these Terms. We provide specialized mechanized wastewater cleaning across Hyderabad:

  • Septic Tank Cleaning: High-vacuum tanker suction for liquid sewage and settled sludge.
  • Drainage & Sewer Jetting: 200–250 bar high-pressure water blasting to clear choked pipelines.
  • STP & Grease Traps: Commercial chamber de-sludging and restaurant grease removal.
  • Basement Drainage: Dewatering flooded pits and cellars (B1/B2/B3).
02

Strictly 100% Mechanized Cleaning

Under the Prohibition of Employment as Manual Scavengers Act, 2013 and government safety mandates:

ZERO HUMAN ENTRY: Our crew will never enter inside any septic tank or sewer chamber. All suction, cleaning, and unclogging are done 100% mechanically from above ground using vacuum hoses and high-pressure jetters.

Booking, Dispatch & Arrival (ETA)

  • 24/7 Fast Dispatch: We operate round the clock. Our target arrival time in Hyderabad is 30 to 45 minutes.
  • Traffic Conditions: Arrival times are realistic operational estimates. Peak traffic, rains, or road restrictions may affect transit. We keep you updated via phone or WhatsApp.
  • Live Location: Sharing your Google live location on WhatsApp helps our driver navigate directly to your gate.
04

Pricing & Payment Terms

  • Clear Rates: Pricing is based on tanker vehicle capacity (e.g. 3,000L to 7,000L) and hose length required.
  • Additional Loads: If your tank holds more volume than one tanker, additional loads are communicated before dispatch and billed at standard rates.
  • Immediate Payment: Payment is due upon completion of cleaning. We accept UPI (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm), Cash, and Direct Bank Transfer.
05

Site Access & Customer Readiness

  • Tanker Access: The customer must ensure clear road passage and parking space for a heavy vacuum tanker (minimum 8–10 ft width).
  • Open Chamber: The septic tank slab or manhole cover must be accessible. If tiled or buried, the customer must arrange for it to be opened.
  • Water Tap: A basic water tap connection is required on-site for line flushing and hydro-jetting operations.
06

DOMESTIC SEWAGE ONLY: We do NOT handle toxic industrial acids, chemical solvents, petroleum/flammable liquids, or medical waste. Customers are strictly liable for any damage or fines resulting from undisclosed hazardous chemicals.
